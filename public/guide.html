1. Video Switching (ATEM Mini)

Top Row (RED = PROGRAM): The camera currently being broadcast to YouTube.

Bottom Row (GREEN = PREVIEW): The next camera or slide cued up.

• [1] Pulpit: Preacher / Center Stage
• [2] Wide: Congregation / Whole Stage
• [3] Proclaim: Lyrics & Sermon Slides

How to switch: Click your next shot in the PREVIEW row, check your multiview, then click ✨ AUTO (Mix) for a smooth crossfade.

🕹️

2. Steering PTZ Cameras

Select Camera: Choose Cam 1 (Pulpit) or Cam 2 (Wide).

Church Presets (Recommended): Click Pulpit, Lectern, Center, or Wide to snap the camera directly to pre-calibrated framing.

Manual Joystick: Click and hold D-pad arrows to pan/tilt; release mouse to stop.

⚠️ Never steer a camera while it is live on PROGRAM! Always cut to another camera first.
🎙️

3. Audio Levels & Sweet Spot

Look at the stereo VU meters on the dashboard:

• -18 to -12 dBFS: Nominal sweet spot for dialogue & music.
• -6 dBFS: Loud peaks (worship band).
• 0 dBFS: Clipping / Distorting (pull fader down).

Silence Watchdog: If audio drops below -50 dB for >30 seconds, the red silence banner flashes. Check the soundboard master fader.

💬

4. Live Speech Captions (VoxStream)

Speech from the pulpit mic appears in real time on the dashboard and feeds the stage teleprompter (port 8765).

• 🧹 Clear Text: Clears current sentence preview.
• 🚨 Panic: Instantly blacks out all captions everywhere if an inappropriate word is transcribed.
• 📱 Stage ↗: Opens the musicians' read-along display.

🚨 EMERGENCY CHEAT SHEET — "IF THIS HAPPENS, DO THAT"

Keep calm. Follow the exact steps below for any broadcast issue.

Volunteer Safeguards
1. RED "SILENCE DETECTED" ALARM FLASHING AUDIO DEAD
  1. Look at the Soundcraft Si Impact soundboard. Is the Main L/R Master Fader up?
  2. Check the USB cable from the soundboard to the broadcast computer.
  3. Check REAPER DAW on the computer. Is the master track muted? (Press M in REAPER to unmute).
2. PTZ CAMERA POINTING AT WALL / WRONG SHOT CAMERA LOST
  1. Immediately cut stream to [3] Proclaim or [2] Wide so viewers don't see the camera moving.
  2. In the PTZ card, select the camera and click Pulpit or Center preset.
  3. Wait 2 seconds for the camera to finish moving, verify in Preview, then cut back.
3. STREAM SAYS "OVERTIME (> 2 HOURS)" YOUTUBE OPEN
  1. Did the service end? If the postlude has finished, tap 🔴 ATEM Stream to stop broadcasting.
  2. If the service is deliberately running long (e.g. baptism/communion), you can leave it streaming.
4. CAPTIONS SHOW WEIRD WORDS / GLITCH CAPTIONS
  1. Click 🧹 Clear Text on the Captions card to wipe the current sentence.
  2. If it keeps glitching, click 🚨 Panic to completely blank the screen until the preacher speaks clearly.
5. CONFIDENCE / STAGE TV MONITOR BLACK MONITOR 1
  1. Click the 📺 Reopen Screen button in the top header.
  2. The system will forcefully re-project OBS Fullscreen to the LONTIUM stage screen and resume speech transcription.
6. ATEM OR OBS SHOWS "OFFLINE" NETWORK
  1. Check the Ethernet cable plugged into the ATEM Mini Pro.
  2. If OBS is closed, launch OBS Studio on the computer desktop. It reconnects automatically.

🛠️ Step-by-Step Installation on Broadcast Computer

macOS & Windows
1 Install Node.js

Download and install Node.js v20+ LTS from https://nodejs.org. Run the installer and accept default settings.

Verification command in Terminal/CMD: node -v
2 Place Folder & Run `npm install`

Place the AV_Controller folder in your desired permanent directory (e.g. C:\AV_Controller on Windows or ~/AV_Controller on Mac).

Open Command Prompt (Windows) or Terminal (Mac) in that folder and run:

cd AV_Controller
npm install
3 Configure REAPER DAW OSC Metering

In REAPER: Options > Preferences > Control/OSC/web > Add:

  • Surface mode: OSC (Open Sound Control)
  • Device IP: 127.0.0.1 | Device Port: 9000
  • Check the box for: "Send feedback"
4 Configure OBS Studio WebSocket v5

In OBS Studio: Tools > WebSocket Server Settings:

  • Check "Enable WebSocket server"
  • Server Port: 4455
5 1-Click Startup & Auto-Boot (Windows & Mac)
🪟 Windows 10/11

1. Fast Setup: Right-click `install.bat` ➔ Run as administrator (auto-installs Node.js via winget & sets firewall).

2. Launch: Double-click `start.bat` or Desktop shortcut.

To shut down: Double-click `stop.bat`.

🍎 macOS

1. Double-click `start.command`.

2. To auto-start as a daemon:

pm2 start server.js --name "sanctuary-av"
pm2 save && pm2 startup
